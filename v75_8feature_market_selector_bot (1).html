Deriv Asian Bot — 8-Feature Research Model + Market Selector

Research build using all eight findings discovered from the uploaded pair data. The model uses a feature score rather than requiring every condition simultaneously. The market is selectable from symbols that Deriv reports as supporting both ASIANU and ASIAND. Default entry is DOWN → UP with a minimum score of 6/8. Flat $0.35 per contract, 5 ticks, 1-second stagger, no Martingale.

Market selection
Default market is V75 (1s). Use “Discover Eligible Markets” after entering your App ID, Demo account ID and PAT. The bot will query Deriv for symbols that support both ASIANU and ASIAND.

Important: The eight-feature thresholds were calibrated from V75 (1s) research. Other markets may have different price scales and volatility. Selecting another market is therefore a research test, not an assumption that the same thresholds transfer unchanged.

The score is deliberately configurable for research. With the default 6/8 threshold, the bot does not require every weak feature to agree on every trade. This is a hypothesis-testing model, not a claim of a persistent edge.

Safety: Demo DOT account only. Exactly two demo purchases per completed pair. No Martingale. The bot will reject a non-Demo DOT account, stop at the pair limit or session-loss limit, and never sends a real-account endpoint.
Entry logic: wait for a DOWN → UP setup on the selected market. Calculate the seven market features plus the previous-pair sequence feature. Enter only when the total score meets the selected threshold. DOWN is bought first, then UP after 1 second.
