Asian Directional Bot — Probabilistic Direction Engine — v28.4

Research build v28.4. Current Deriv public market-data WebSocket + authenticated account trading stream for Demo or Real Options accounts. One ASIANU/ASIAND trade at a time. Core direction requires Price vs previous-5 SMA + SMA slope + Momentum 3 to agree 3/3. SMA-side persistence is a configurable anti-noise filter. Directional normalized distance and normalized SMA slope are recorded for research; volatility and acceleration remain contextual, not hard filters. Volatility is contextual only. Rolling win rate is advisory in BALANCED mode. Winning-streak persistence affects sizing only. Loss progression and loss-streak protection are separate risk controls.

Deriv account connection
Account mode: DEMO. The bot will request a Demo-scoped authenticated WebSocket.
Strategy & entry settings

V28.4 selective direction engine: UP and DOWN are scored by separate candidate-direction logistic models using direction-relative features. The live selector uses the raw model probabilities for direction and keeps calibrated probabilities as diagnostics only. A live entry requires balanced model warm-up, the payout-aware break-even floor, the configured raw probability threshold and confidence gap, then the market-quality gates.

V28.4 calibration: empirical calibration remains recorded for research, but it is not used to select live direction. This avoids the degradation observed when the calibration layer compressed the useful raw probability ranking in the prior run.

V28.4 market-quality gates: when enabled, live probability trades require distance at or above the minimum, volatility at or below the maximum, and direction-specific handling in weakening trends. DOWN is blocked in WEAKENING TREND by default; UP requires a higher probability threshold there. These gates are configurable and are also recorded for every research sample.

Research integrity: research samples are non-overlapping by default. A new research sample is not created while another is awaiting settlement, and configured spacing cannot be shorter than the expiry window. Each decisive expiry path scores both hypothetical directions on the same future price path. UP and DOWN models are trained independently, and each direction must meet its own minimum sample requirement before live probability selection is enabled.

V26 research design: every qualifying setup is evaluated under the same future 5-tick Asian settlement window. The live candidates remain Technical baseline, Acceleration-confirmed, Acceleration-reversal follow, and Contrarian exhaustion. The 2/4 Strategy Consensus engine now uses independent directional opinions from all four strategy theses on every qualifying technical setup, even when a strategy's original live-entry conditions are not met. At least 2 votes are required, a 2–2 tie is rejected, and 3/4 or 4/4 consensus qualifies. Candidate-entry conditions remain unchanged for the individual strategies. Optional consensus filters can block opposing votes, filter acceleration state, cap distance, filter volatility relative to its rolling baseline, and pause after consecutive consensus losses. Each consensus voter may return UP, DOWN, or NO OPINION; abstentions do not count as opposing votes. Random remains a research/control strategy and is not a live consensus voter. The default remains flat $0.35 validation with no martingale.

Independent consensus opinion model: Each voter can say UP, DOWN, or NO OPINION. Technical gives its technical direction. Acceleration-Confirmed gives the technical direction only when acceleration strengthens it. Acceleration-Reversal Follow gives the technical direction only when acceleration weakens/reverses. Contrarian Exhaustion gives the opposite direction only when exhaustion conditions are active. Abstentions are ignored in the vote. These opinions are used only by the consensus engine; the original live candidate conditions remain separately recorded.

V27 research comparison: Every research sample also evaluates a hypothetical raw 2/4 consensus + STRENGTHENING acceleration variant. This is research-only: it does not change the live consensus decision, filters, or execution. It is recorded alongside raw consensus and filtered consensus outcomes so acceleration strengthening can be tested independently on the same 5-tick settlement window.

Rolling win rate & streak persistence
SMA side persistence is an entry-quality filter; normalized slope and signed distance are recorded for research only. Persistence is direction-specific: UP→UP→UP is an UP streak; DOWN→DOWN→DOWN is a DOWN streak. Changing direction resets the current winning streak for sizing. The persistence statistic is P(next win | N prior same-direction wins).
Loss progression — adjustable

Next-trade stake after L consecutive losses = min(base × factor^min(L,max level), cap). Set the factor to 1.00 for flat staking. A win resets the loss progression to base.

Loss-streak protection

Research samples: every configured number of ticks, when no live trade is active, the bot captures the current setup and later settles the selected strategy, technical baseline, opposite direction, continuation candidate, exhaustion candidate and deterministic random-control direction on the same future tick window. This is research-only.

Cooldown records: when cooldown protection is active, each qualifying signal is recorded as a paper trade by default. It is never sent to Deriv, never changes rolling WR or streak state, and is settled after the configured paper duration using the Asian Up/Down rule (exit tick versus the average of the contract-period ticks).

Connection resilience: market data is tested end-to-end (history + subscription + newer tick) before RUNNING; the bot also logs endpoint, WebSocket errors, close codes, rejected requests, and uncaught JavaScript errors. If the idle authenticated trading socket drops, the bot automatically reconnects up to 3 times before stopping. If a contract is active when the trading socket drops, the bot stops safely and never opens a replacement trade.

The base entry rule follows the selected core confirmation mode + selected numeric distance threshold. After 2 losses, protection upgrades the core requirement to 3/3 + distance. After 3 losses, Momentum 5 must also agree. At 4 losses, trading pauses until 5 distinct valid signal events have appeared. At 5 losses, the session stops. When Session target profit is above $0, the bot stops opening new trades once cumulative session P/L reaches the target. The currently open contract is allowed to settle normally.

Optional warm-start history

Imported records are used only for rolling WR and persistence calculations. They are stored by market. Historical records from a different strategy are not proof of current behavior.

Account safety: select DEMO or REAL deliberately. REAL mode requires the explicit arming checkbox before START. The PAT is used only in page memory and is never written to localStorage. Market data is read from a separate public WebSocket; trading uses an authenticated account-scoped WebSocket for the selected account. If either connection drops, the bot stops rather than opening another trade.
V28.4 decision flow: raw probability → break-even + confidence → market-quality gates → UP / DOWN / NO TRADE. Calibration is research-only. Threshold sweep records 60%, 65%, 70%, and 75% raw-probability variants on every settled research sample.
Decision flow: feature vector → probabilistic UP/DOWN estimates → probability threshold + confidence gap → independent risk controls → at most one directional trade. Technical, consensus and contrarian modules remain available for comparison and are used as model inputs rather than equal-weight voters. When 2/4 STRATEGY CONSENSUS is selected, all four strategy theses independently return UP, DOWN, or NO OPINION on the same setup. A strict majority of at least 2 is required; 2–2 ties and fewer-than-2 votes are rejected. Consensus-specific opposing-vote, acceleration, distance, volatility, and loss-guard settings apply only to that live strategy. Random remains research-only. V26 also reports both actual execution P/L and a normalized flat-stake signal P/L so money-management effects can be separated from signal performance.
