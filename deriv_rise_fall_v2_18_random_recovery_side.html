Deriv Rise/Fall KNN Bot V2.18 — Two-Side After Every Loss

Rise/Fall • calibrated KNN • soft mean-reversion regime • payout-aware recovery • two-side reset after every normal-trade loss. API mapping: Rise=PUT, Fall=CALL.

Survival allocation—
Online sequence learning: the directional KNN remains the primary prediction engine. A separate learner watches only normal trade wins/losses and learns 1–4 step outcome patterns, plus direction-specific patterns. It gently adjusts the KNN candidate probability when enough live observations exist. Two-side reset legs are deliberately excluded so they do not contaminate directional sequence learning. Sequence history is stored locally by market + duration so the learner can continue across demo sessions.
Two-side recovery: after every normal-trade loss, the bot opens RISE + FALL. One side is randomly selected to receive the configurable recovery factor; the other side stays at base stake. After the pair settles, the next normal trade is at base stake. A normal WIN exits reset mode; a normal LOSS starts another randomized two-sided recovery pair. The selected recovery side is re-randomized for every pair.
Calibration bootstrap: until a probability bucket has enough out-of-sample observations, the model preserves most of the current signal instead of collapsing it toward 50%. This prevents a cold-start "zero trades" condition while empirical calibration builds.
The survival engine treats the sequence-loss budget as the hard risk boundary. After a loss it calculates the payout-aware recovery stake, then caps that stake so the remaining sequence budget is still sufficient to absorb the configured number of consecutive losses. A win resets the sequence. Session profit/loss limits remain separate hard stops.

Entry logic: Market ticks use a dedicated public WebSocket; trading uses a separate authenticated WebSocket. Both connections are monitored and automatically reconnected while the session is active. SMA(5) slope + Momentum 3/5 + normalized distance + acceleration + volatility → KNN P(RISE)/P(FALL) → walk-forward calibration → choose higher-probability side → request a live Deriv proposal → calculate actual payout → enter only when probability and payout pass the configured thresholds. After a loss, the survival engine computes the stake needed to recover the accumulated loss plus the recovery target at the live payout, then caps it against the remaining sequence-loss budget, configured budget cap, and hard USD cap. For ordinary trades it never forces an opposite direction; the model still chooses the direction independently. After the loss-streak trigger, the dedicated two-side reset intentionally buys both directions at the base stake.

Stable connection layer: market and trade sockets are independent, trade OTP is renewed on reconnect, subscriptions are restored, heartbeats are monitored, open positions are reconciled after trade-socket interruptions, and the sequence-loss budget is entered directly by the user for the risk engine.
