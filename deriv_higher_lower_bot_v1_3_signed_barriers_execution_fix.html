Connection: the authenticated trading socket is opened from Deriv's one-time OTP URL; no second PAT authorization message is sent after the socket opens.
Barrier model: HIGHER and LOWER are now evaluated independently across barriers on both sides of the current price. For example, HIGHER at a barrier below spot can have a materially higher terminal probability, while LOWER at a barrier above spot can do the same.
Execution profiles: Balanced is the normal starting profile. Strict restores the original 60% probability / positive-EV / 20–200% payout gate. Test — proposal only intentionally bypasses probability, payout and EV filters so we can verify proposal pricing and paper execution.
V1 logic: estimate the probability that the terminal price will finish above or below each candidate relative barrier after the configured tick duration. Historical outcomes are weighted by recency and adjusted with current momentum, slope, persistence, and acceleration. The bot requests live Deriv proposals for the strongest candidates, then ranks them by expected value using the live quoted payout.
Trade filter: model probability must be inside the configured minimum/maximum range, quoted payout must be inside the minimum/maximum payout band, and EV must be above the configured threshold.
Recovery: payout-aware recovery uses the actual quoted return. A win resets recovery. Session profit/loss stops operate independently from the recovery target.
