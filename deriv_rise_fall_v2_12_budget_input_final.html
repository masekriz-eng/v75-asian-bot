Entry logic: Market ticks use a dedicated public WebSocket; trading uses a separate authenticated WebSocket. Both connections are monitored and automatically reconnected while the session is active. SMA(5) slope + Momentum 3/5 + normalized distance + acceleration + volatility → KNN P(RISE)/P(FALL) → walk-forward calibration → choose higher-probability side → request a live Deriv proposal → calculate actual payout → enter only when probability and payout pass the configured thresholds. After a loss, the survival engine computes the stake needed to recover the accumulated loss plus the recovery target at the live payout, then caps it against the remaining sequence-loss budget, configured budget cap, and hard USD cap. It never forces an opposite direction; the model still chooses the direction independently.

Stable connection layer: market and trade sockets are independent, trade OTP is renewed on reconnect, subscriptions are restored, heartbeats are monitored, open positions are reconciled after trade-socket interruptions, and the sequence-loss budget is entered directly by the user for the risk engine. V2.12 keeps the same 9 scale-normalized model features and walk-forward outcome embargo from the uploaded V2.9.1 base. The new risk layer is intentionally separate from the signal layer: it can cap or stop exposure without changing the model direction.
