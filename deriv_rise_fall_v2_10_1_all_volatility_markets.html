Entry logic: Market ticks use a dedicated public WebSocket; trading uses a separate authenticated WebSocket. Both connections are monitored and automatically reconnected while the session is active. SMA(5) slope + Momentum 3/5 + normalized distance + acceleration + volatility → KNN P(RISE)/P(FALL) → walk-forward calibration → choose higher-probability side → request a live Deriv proposal → calculate actual profit-on-stake payout → enter only when probability and payout both pass their thresholds. After a loss, the next model-approved trade can increase stake according to the selected martingale mode; a win resets to base. The model still chooses direction independently—no forced opposite trade.

Volatility markets: the Market dropdown now includes the full Volatility set used by this bot, including the 5/10/15/25/30/50/75/90/100 families and their 1-second variants where available, plus Volatility 150 (1s) and 250 (1s). The actual Deriv active_symbols/contracts_for response remains authoritative for whether a specific market is currently available for the selected option contract type.
Loss protection / auto-switch: after 2 consecutive losses the bot pauses new entries. With auto-switch ON, it compares the latest 15 completed strategy trades for each configured market. It switches only to a different market that has at least 15 recorded trades and a higher rolling win rate. Otherwise the bot remains paused.
Parallel monitoring is the live market-selection layer: all configured markets receive live ticks at the same time. Only the selected market can place real trades; every other market runs the same strategy as paper monitoring and contributes completed outcomes to its rolling 15-trade performance.
Pre-start cross-market warm-up: when enabled, the selected market is not traded initially. The bot runs the same strategy in paper mode on the other configured markets, rotating through them one at a time, until the required number of completed warm-up trades is reached. Warm-up results are recorded for market-performance analysis, but do not affect session P/L, martingale recovery, consecutive-loss state, or the live session trade count. The bot then returns to the originally selected market, rebuilds its model, and starts normal trading.
Stable connection layer: market and trade sockets are independent, trade OTP is renewed on reconnect, subscriptions are restored, heartbeats are monitored, and open positions are reconciled after trade-socket interruptions. V2.9 model: 9 scale-normalized features (SMA distance/slope, M1/M3/M5, acceleration, volatility ratios, persistence, shock). KNN uses standardized feature distance + recency weighting. Walk-forward calibration uses an outcome embargo so labels whose expiry is not yet reached cannot enter a historical prediction. Entries also require reasonable neighbor agreement and distance, using an adaptive Balanced/Strict/Explore profile so the model cannot silently over-restrict itself.
