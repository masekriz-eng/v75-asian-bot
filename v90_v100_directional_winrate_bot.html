Deriv Asian Direction Bot — Rolling Win Rate

Clean single-direction research/trading build for Volatility 90 / Volatility 100 and other markets that expose both ASIANU and ASIAND. The engine uses the direction with the higher historical win rate, provided the selected market has enough prior observations and the win-rate gap clears the confidence threshold.

1. Demo connection
The page sends the PAT directly to Deriv only to request a short-lived authenticated WebSocket URL. It is not stored in localStorage.
2. Market
Default market: V75 (1s). Discovering markets is optional; the selected market will be fully revalidated before trading.
Data integrity: waiting for selected-market history.
3. Direction engine
Entry rule: use the last 20 completed directional trades for the selected market. Both directions must have at least 10 observations. The bot selects DOWN when DOWN win rate exceeds UP by at least 5.0 percentage points; it selects UP under the reverse condition. A tie or smaller gap means NO TRADE.
4. Money management
When ON, consecutive losses move the next stake through $0.35 → $0.40 → $0.46 → $0.53 → $0.61 → $0.70. Any win resets to $0.35. This is a capped progression, not a guarantee of recovery.
5. Seed / persist directional history
No imported history is required, but the engine will not trade until the minimum directional observations are available for the selected market.
