Deriv Asian Bot — 3 Mandatory + 1 Optional Feature

Research build using the three mandatory conditions identified from the uploaded V75 pair data, plus one selectable optional feature. The market is selectable from symbols that Deriv reports as supporting both ASIANU and ASIAND. Flat $0.35 base stake, 5 ticks, 1-second stagger, optional capped money management.

Deriv Demo connection
Market selection
Default market is V75 (1s). Use “Discover Eligible Markets” after entering your App ID, Demo account ID and PAT. The bot will query Deriv for symbols that support both ASIANU and ASIAND.
Data integrity: waiting for a clean market history.

Important: The research thresholds were calibrated from V75 (1s) data. Other markets may have different price scales and volatility; selecting another market is therefore a separate research test.

Trade & test settings
When ON: after a BOTH-LOSS pair, the next pair advances through $0.40 → $0.46 → $0.53 → $0.61 → $0.70 maximum. BOTH WIN or MIXED resets to $0.35. The progression is capped and does not guarantee recovery.
Entry model
MANDATORY 1 — Direction
Current tick must be below the previous-5-tick SMA → DOWN first, then UP.
MANDATORY 2 — SMA Slope
SMA slope must be negative.
MANDATORY 3 — Volatility
20-tick standard deviation ≥ 1.00.
OPTIONAL 4 — Select one
Choose one of the remaining five features in Trade & Test Settings. The selected feature must pass before a pair is entered. “None” disables the extra filter.
Remaining options: 3-tick momentum, SMA distance, 5-tick momentum, momentum acceleration, or previous-pair sequence.

Research design: Entry is a clean AND rule: all three mandatory conditions must pass, plus the selected optional condition when enabled. The SMA-slope test is sign-based and the volatility test is calibrated to the selected market's recent median, so the mandatory rules do not fail simply because another volatility index has a different raw scale. Money management is independent of the signal logic and can be switched ON/OFF before each test. All eight feature values and money-management state are logged to CSV.

Safety: Demo DOT account only. Exactly two demo purchases per completed pair. The bot will reject a non-Demo DOT account, clear old tick subscriptions before every test, load a fresh history for the selected market, verify the live tick stream belongs to that market, and stop at the pair limit or session-loss limit.
Entry logic: all three mandatory conditions must pass: DOWN → UP direction, SMA slope ≤ -0.20, and stdev20 ≥ 1.00. One optional feature may also be selected. DOWN is bought first, then UP after 1 second.
