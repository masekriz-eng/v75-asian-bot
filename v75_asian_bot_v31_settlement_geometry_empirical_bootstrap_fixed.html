Asian Directional Bot — V31 Settlement Geometry + Empirical Path — Progressive Loss-Streak Firewall

Research build V31. Current Deriv public market-data WebSocket + authenticated account trading stream for Demo or Real Options accounts. One ASIANU/ASIAND trade at a time. V31 is an empirical settlement-geometry strategy: it learns from strictly chronological, non-overlapping five-tick Asian outcomes using the recent pre-entry tick path, signed displacement, slope, acceleration and volatility buckets. It uses hierarchical pattern backoff, Bayesian smoothing, payout-aware expected value, and a progressive loss-streak firewall. Strategy & entry settings
Walk-forward adaptive controls

V31: train → freeze → test → retrain, with drift protection and a progressive loss-streak firewall during the frozen test phase. GBM, KNN and Markov are trained during an initial warm-up, then frozen for a test block. New outcomes stay held out until a scheduled walk-forward checkpoint or block boundary. During testing, a rolling monitor compares recent realized WR with the average probability assigned by the frozen ensemble, and separately checks UP and DOWN. A drift warning requires the configured probability-vs-realized gap; confirmation requires repeated degraded checks. Confirmed UP/DOWN drift now pauses only that direction immediately; directional drift does not retrain or reset the frozen block. Overall confirmed drift can still pause all entries and use the optional early-retrain path. Each direction has its own confirmation streak, and each frozen model captures the test-block target at freeze time so the block completes at exactly the configured number of settled live trades (25 by default), unless an overall confirmed drift explicitly triggers early recovery. The frozen models do not update after individual live outcomes.

V30.5.1 progressive frozen-evidence rules: once a walk-forward block freezes, all WF model/decision hyperparameters are captured with it. The live dashboard and LIVE CSV record the exact frozen GBM/KNN/Markov probabilities used at entry. At loss-firewall levels 2–5, all three frozen models must independently clear the level threshold; the weakest aligned model is binding. A strong opposing frozen KNN prediction can veto an otherwise valid 2-of-3 ensemble decision at the configured threshold (65% by default). After 2/3/4/5 consecutive losses, the loss firewall raises the live-entry bar to the configured defensive or ultra-defensive gates and can reject the next signal without affecting the frozen model. Directional drift pauses only the confirmed direction; block length remains the captured target.

V30.5.1 progressive loss-firewall rules: global and direction-specific loss streaks drive progressive entry gates at 2/3/4/5 losses. The firewall applies to walk-forward live decisions, does not alter model training or stake size, and hard-stops at 6 losses by default.

It now uses the already-computed active model snapshot direction, avoiding the s is not defined failure that could abort market-data startup. V30.5.1 keeps the V30.4 firewall/KNN/drift architecture while enforcing strict per-model defensive evidence and correcting protection-state reporting.

V29.2 Markov Regime-Conditioned Direction Engine: the live direction model compares the most recent normalized 12-tick path against previously settled, chronological research paths. The Markov engine conditions on the recent UP/DOWN/flat tick-state sequence and current regime, then backs off from specific sequences to shorter suffixes, regime-only, and global history. Bayesian smoothing reduces the impact of sparse contexts. Live entry requires Markov warm-up, the payout-aware break-even floor, the configured probability threshold and confidence gap, then the existing market-quality gates.

V29.2 validation design: V28 raw logistic probability remains available as a benchmark and is trained in parallel, but it does not drive V29 live entries. GBM predictions are not calibrated or manually reweighted for live use. Their threshold performance is recorded separately on the same future 5-tick outcome.

V29.2 market-quality gates: when enabled, live KNN trades require distance at or above the minimum, volatility at or below the maximum, and direction-specific handling in weakening trends. DOWN is blocked in WEAKENING TREND by default; UP requires a higher probability threshold there. Research samples are non-overlapping by default. V31 is trained only after its full 5-tick outcome settles; the V30/V29 benchmark engines retain their existing held-out training rules.

V30.5.1 research design: the research engine evaluates V29.1 KNN, V29 GBM, the V28 raw probability benchmark, the technical baseline, opposite direction, acceleration variants, contrarian exhaustion and random control on the same future 5-tick path. The active KNN and GBM models are trained only from completed research outcomes. Research samples are separated by the expiry horizon so one outcome window cannot overlap the next.

Rolling win rate & streak persistence
SMA side persistence is an entry-quality filter; normalized slope and signed distance are recorded for research only. Persistence is direction-specific: UP→UP→UP is an UP streak; DOWN→DOWN→DOWN is a DOWN streak. Changing direction resets the current winning streak for sizing. The persistence statistic is P(next win | N prior same-direction wins).
Loss progression — adjustable

Next-trade stake after L consecutive losses = min(base × factor^min(L,max level), cap). Set the factor to 1.00 for flat staking. A win resets the loss progression to base.

Loss-streak firewall — V30.5.1

Architecture: 0–1 losses = NORMAL; 2 = DEFENSIVE 1; 3 = DEFENSIVE 2; 4 = ULTRA DEFENSIVE 1; 5 = ULTRA DEFENSIVE 2; 6 = HARD STOP. Defensive probability and gap gates are binding per frozen model: the weakest aligned model sets the effective confidence. The effective defense level is the maximum of the global consecutive-loss streak and, when directional guard is ON, the selected direction's consecutive-loss streak. A win resets the global streak and the winning direction's directional streak.

After 2 losses: require 3/3 frozen walk-forward directional agreement, ensemble probability ≥70%, weakest aligned model ≥60%, ensemble gap ≥12pp, distance ≥0.80σ and KNN opposition below 65%. After 3: 3/3 agreement, ensemble probability ≥75%, weakest aligned model ≥65%, ensemble gap ≥15pp, distance ≥1.00σ, KNN opposition below 60%, plus momentum-5 and acceleration support. After 4: 3/3 agreement, ensemble probability ≥80%, weakest aligned model ≥70%, ensemble gap ≥18pp, distance ≥1.20σ, KNN opposition below 55%, supportive continuation regime, and no drift warning. After 5: 3/3 agreement, ensemble probability ≥85%, weakest aligned model ≥75%, ensemble gap ≥20pp, distance ≥1.50σ, KNN must agree, supportive continuation regime, and no drift warning. These gates are applied to V30 walk-forward live decisions instead of being bypassed by probability mode. Benchmark single-model modes use their own model probability/gap as the equivalent evidence.

V30.5.1 strict-evidence fix: normal walk-forward trading may still use 2/3 agreement, but once the loss firewall reaches level 2+, the firewall re-evaluates the exact frozen GBM/KNN/Markov snapshots independently. A 60% model may satisfy the Level-2 weakest-model floor, but it cannot hide inside the ensemble: the ensemble itself must still meet the higher level threshold and the required 3/3 directional agreement must be genuine. The minimum aligned model probability and minimum aligned model gap are logged for auditability.

Safety behavior: reaching the defensive levels does not increase the stake. The firewall only rejects entries. Cooldown remains available for legacy consensus mode; the V30 loss firewall no longer uses an automatic pause-after-4-losses rule. The session hard-stops at the configured loss count (6 by default).

V31 settlement geometry + empirical path strategy

V31 warm-start: the bot bootstraps V31 from the current market's historical ticks using non-overlapping 5-tick outcomes. This removes the circular startup condition where V31 had to wait for live research samples before it could make its first live decision. Live outcomes continue to be learned chronologically after startup.

V31 research target: each sample stores the pre-entry setup and then waits for the next five ticks. The outcome target is the Asian UP/DOWN settlement condition from those five future ticks. The model never adds that future outcome until the sample settles, preventing same-sample leakage. Pattern hierarchy backs off from exact pattern + signed distance + slope + acceleration + volatility to broader pattern buckets and finally the global base rate.

Payout-aware filter: EV = P(win) × net payout − P(loss). The model computes the break-even probability and a required probability for the configured minimum EV. Keep V31 net payout equal to the actual current contract quote before using live money; an incorrect payout makes the EV filter invalid.

Firewall: 0–1 losses NORMAL; 2 DEFENSIVE 1; 3 DEFENSIVE 2; 4 ULTRA DEFENSIVE 1; 5 ULTRA DEFENSIVE 2; 6 HARD STOP. The V31 firewall only raises entry requirements; it never increases the stake. The firewall can also reject a V31 candidate after 2–5 losses while the underlying model keeps learning from held-out research outcomes.

V29.2 decision flow: Markov probability → break-even + confidence → market-quality gates → UP / DOWN / NO TRADE. V29 GBM, V29.1 KNN and V28 raw probability remain parallel benchmarks. Threshold sweeps for both models are recorded on every settled research sample. GBM threshold variants are kept as shadow controls; only the configured V29 GBM selector can place live trades.
Decision flow: feature vector → gradient-boosted nonlinear direction estimate → probability threshold + confidence gap → independent risk controls → at most one directional trade. Technical, consensus and contrarian modules remain available for comparison and are used as model inputs rather than equal-weight voters. When 2/4 STRATEGY CONSENSUS is selected, all four strategy theses independently return UP, DOWN, or NO OPINION on the same setup. A strict majority of at least 2 is required; 2–2 ties and fewer-than-2 votes are rejected. Consensus-specific opposing-vote, acceleration, distance, volatility, and loss-guard settings apply only to that live strategy. Random remains research-only. V26 also reports both actual execution P/L and a normalized flat-stake signal P/L so money-management effects can be separated from signal performance.

V30.1 drift protection: a rolling test window is evaluated only at the configured checkpoint interval. Direction-specific deterioration can pause one side without disabling the other. Overall confirmed deterioration can pause all new entries. In the default response, the bot performs a controlled checkpoint retrain using held-out research outcomes and freezes the replacement model before resuming. Open contracts are allowed to settle normally.
