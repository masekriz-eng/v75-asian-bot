Asian Directional Bot — V35.0 Settlement-Margin Dynamic Double-Side

V35.0 settlement-margin prediction research/live build. Each qualifying V35 signal opens two contracts: the model-predicted side at the configured probability-tier factor and the opposite side at exactly 1.00× base stake. The prediction engine does not directly classify UP/DOWN; it predicts the future Asian settlement margin — final tick minus the average of the preceding settlement-window ticks — normalized by entry tick-step volatility. The predicted continuous margin is converted to P(UP)/P(DOWN) using learned residual uncertainty. V35 is independently trained and persisted by market and does not depend on the V34 final probability, reliability adjustment or V34 trajectory gate. The 5-second configurable BOTH-SIDE LOSS delay remains active. V34.9.11 remains available as a benchmark.

Deriv account connection
Account mode: DEMO. The bot will request a Demo-scoped authenticated WebSocket.

V35.0 Core controls: margin prediction, model activation, probability tiers and volatility. All loss-streak entry defenses and same-side loss guards are disabled in the active weighted path. Legacy benchmark/research controls are hidden by default.

Strategy & entry settings

V32 live architecture: in V32 mode, 2/3 core, SMA persistence, distance, slope, acceleration, momentum and regime are predictive inputs rather than separate live vetoes. The primary live gates are calibrated model probability, calibrated confidence gap, payout-aware EV, model warm-up and a single extreme-volatility safety check. Loss-streak protection remains independent and is still enforced.

V35.0 Settlement-Margin Prediction

New prediction mechanism: V35 does not classify the contract outcome directly. It predicts the future settlement margin — final tick minus the average of the preceding settlement-window ticks — in standardized Z units. The predicted margin and learned residual uncertainty are then converted into P(UP) and P(DOWN). This keeps the double-side factor tied to the model's predicted settlement distance rather than only to a binary classifier.

Training target: for a 5-tick contract, the target is final tick − mean(first 4 ticks), normalized by the entry tick-step volatility. The actual outcome is never used until settlement. V35 is independently trainable/persisted and does not use the V34 final probability, reliability adjustment, trajectory gate, or V34 calibration as its prediction target.

V34.8.2 entry architecture: V34.8.2 keeps the candidate-direction trajectory gate and adds an out-of-sample reliability layer: the V34 probability trajectory must be STABLE or RISING in the selected candidate direction. For UP, rising P(UP) is strengthening; for DOWN, falling P(UP) is strengthening. STABLE is acceptable for either direction. FALLING/WEAKENING setups are blocked. The existing calibrated dynamic-probability threshold remains the first requirement.

V34 architecture: V34 preserves V33 as a benchmark and adds a separate dynamic-probability meta-model. It uses the instantaneous V33 raw probability before V33 sample shrinkage, recent probability trajectory, run-start drift, trajectory slope/acceleration/persistence, source agreement, KNN neighborhood quality, regime, distance, slope, momentum, acceleration and volatility change. The dynamic probability is sample-shrunk and calibrated before the entry gate. Rising/falling probability is a learned feature, not a hard veto.

V34.1 Dynamic Rescue diagnostics: rescue remains research-only and never overrides the V34.8.2 reliability-adjusted + stable/rising live gate. V34.1 separately tracks a shadow rescue universe where the V33 candidate calibrated probability is 55%–<72%. A setup is marked RESCUE only when V34 is warmed, keeps the same candidate direction, raises the calibrated probability by at least the configured lift, and reaches the rescue dynamic-calibration floor. Rescue is research-only and never bypasses the live V34 gate.

V34.8.2 profile: balanced-conservative probability/EV requirements, 40/20/20 model warm-up floors, moderate learning and regularization, conservative calibration/reliability smoothing, a 6-tick trajectory lookback, and a 1.75× volatility cap. V34.8 keeps these defaults while upgrading the predictive feature representation; they do not guarantee profitability.

V34.8.1 calibration stabilization: the first 42 features remain the legacy V34 base model and the remaining V34.8 predictive-core features form the incremental block. The incremental block is still learned, but receives a stronger L2 penalty and its total contribution is capped at the configured number of percentage points around the base-model probability. The cap is also enforced after calibration. The uncapped incremental effect is retained only for research diagnostics.

V34.9.5 live-model tuning: the V34 meta-model learns from a 79-feature vector. Conditional probabilities remain Beta-smoothed evidence features. V34.5 adds eight reliability features and a separate out-of-sample reliability adjustment to the final calibrated probability. Reliability only shrinks an overconfident model; it never boosts probabilities. The direct V34 knobs are minimum probability, minimum EV, learning rate/L2, calibration/reliability controls, new-feature controls, trajectory controls and the extreme-volatility cap. Live entry no longer has a fixed 40-sample warm-up.

V34.8 predictive-core additions: the 79-feature model adds nonlinear distance zones, candidate-vs-M3/M5/acceleration reversal relationships, a reversal score, source-ensemble vs random-walk edge, V33-vs-random edge, direction-specific probability-bucket evidence, probability-band nonlinearities and distance×reversal interactions. These are learned inputs, not extra hard entry vetoes. Direction×regime evidence remains available through the conditional layer. Loss-streak entry safety and same-side loss guards are disabled for the active sequential V34.9.7 path.

Background/benchmark controls: controls used only by V28/V29/V31/V32/V33 or research diagnostics remain in the DOM for benchmark compatibility but are not V34.8 direct tuning knobs. V34.5 rescue parameters are now hidden because rescue is research-only. Loss-safety controls that still affect V34 live entries remain visible below.

V33 architecture: V33 extends V32 with probability trajectory, pairwise KNN/GBM/V28 agreement, KNN-neighborhood quality, regime phase, volatility change, nonlinear interactions, empirical setup priors, candidate-direction persistence, and sample-size confidence shrinkage. These are predictive inputs, not stacked hard vetoes. V33 confidence shrinkage starts at 50% when the model has little evidence, then grows with settled samples; calibration blends that estimate with observed probability/regime outcomes.

V33 baseline: probability ≥72%, EV ≥0, 150 total settled samples, 60 UP + 60 DOWN minimums, L2=0.01, calibration prior=25, confidence prior=100, extreme volatility cap 2.00×. Confidence gap is logged but not used as a duplicate gate because P(DOWN)=1−P(UP).

V32 test baseline: live strategy = V32 calibrated model-driven ensemble; core confirmation = 2/3 (predictive input, not a V32 veto); SMA side persistence = 1 tick (predictive input, not a V32 veto); distance reference = 0.40σ (informational in V32); probability floor = 75%; confidence gap = 9pp; minimum EV = 0; V32 warm-up = 60 settled samples; 25 UP + 25 DOWN minimums; extreme volatility guard = 2.00× baseline. Loss progression and the existing loss-streak firewall remain enabled independently.

V32 model-driven calibrated ensemble: the predictor learns one P(UP) distribution from KNN path probability, GBM probability, V28 probability, source-model disagreement, regime, distance, slope, momentum, acceleration, core agreement and SMA persistence. It then applies global and regime-specific shrinkage calibration. Live V32 entry uses only calibrated probability, confidence gap, payout-aware EV, model warm-up and the optional extreme-volatility guard; the technical features are not separate hard vetoes. Rejected candidate directions are retained as research shadows so we can measure the opportunity cost of filtering.

V30.1 validation design: V28 raw logistic probability remains available as a benchmark and is trained in parallel, but it does not drive V29 live entries. GBM predictions are not calibrated or manually reweighted for live use. Their threshold performance is recorded separately on the same future 5-tick outcome.

V30.1 market-quality gates: when enabled, live KNN trades require distance at or above the minimum, volatility at or below the maximum, and direction-specific handling in weakening trends. DOWN is blocked in WEAKENING TREND by default; UP requires a higher probability threshold there. Research samples are non-overlapping by default and the KNN is trained only after the 5-tick outcome settles.

V30.1 research design: the research engine evaluates V30.1 KNN, V29 GBM, the V28 raw probability benchmark, the technical baseline, opposite direction, acceleration variants, contrarian exhaustion and random control on the same future 5-tick path. The active KNN and GBM models are trained only from completed research outcomes. Research samples are separated by the expiry horizon so one outcome window cannot overlap the next.

Rolling win rate & streak persistence
SMA side persistence is a legacy hard filter for non-V32 strategies; V32 treats persistence, slope and signed distance as model inputs and research variables. Persistence is direction-specific: UP→UP→UP is an UP streak; DOWN→DOWN→DOWN is a DOWN streak. Changing direction resets the current winning streak for sizing. The persistence statistic is P(next win | N prior same-direction wins).
Loss progression — adjustable

Next-trade stake after L consecutive losses = min(base × factor^min(L,max level), cap). Set the factor to 1.00 for flat staking. A win resets the loss progression to base.

V34.9.11 entry policy: loss-streak entry safety, hard-stop-by-loss-count, and same-side loss guards are disabled for the active sequential model. The model is blocked only by its own signal-quality gates, the explicit both-side-loss delay, session loss stop, max-trades limit, and operational connection/account safety. A recovery trade is still permitted only after a confirmed LOSS.

Legacy loss-streak entry safety + same-side loss guard — inactive in V34.9.11

Same-side loss guard: after 2 live losses on the same direction, a third trade in that direction is blocked even when all normal V34 entry gates pass. One live trade in the opposite direction is treated as the recovery trade; once it settles, the blocked side is unlocked whether the recovery trade wins or loses. Research-shadow candidates remain recorded and do not change the live guard state.

V34 model memory: trained model components are stored locally by market and restored on the next run. A fresh model uses an equal-weight neutral cold-start basis rather than defaulting to UP.

V32 loss ladder: 0–1 losses = NORMAL; 2 = DEFENSIVE 1; 3 = DEFENSIVE 2; 4 = ULTRA DEFENSIVE 1; 5 = ULTRA DEFENSIVE 2; 6 = HARD STOP. In V32 mode the firewall uses the calibrated V32 probability, a modest volatility cap and a small stability requirement; it does not require technical core agreement, Momentum-5, strengthening regime, acceleration agreement, or a separate distance floor. In V34 mode, the additional loss-streak overlay uses the same calibrated-probability standard as the main V34 gate and, by default, requires the trajectory to be STABLE or RISING in the candidate direction. Confidence-gap and distance fields remain only for legacy non-V32 modes. The legacy KNN recovery path is disabled in V32. Stability confirmations are consecutive valid V32 candidate evaluations after the latest settled trade; a non-qualifying V32 setup resets the confirmation count.

V34.9.5 weighted loss safety: after 2+ losses, weighted mode now uses the V34 model-driven path: minimum calibrated probability, minimum EV, candidate-direction STABLE/RISING trajectory and the normal V34 extreme-volatility cap. Legacy KNN confidence-gap and distance floors are not used by the weighted strategy.

Shadow safety audit: when the firewall blocks a KNN candidate, the candidate is retained as a research-only shadow outcome so we can measure rejected winners versus rejected losers. No firewall-blocked candidate can enter the live account or change the live loss streak.

Connection resilience: market data is tested end-to-end (history + subscription + newer tick) before RUNNING; the bot logs endpoint, WebSocket errors, close codes, rejected requests, and uncaught JavaScript errors. If the idle authenticated trading socket drops, the bot automatically reconnects up to 3 times before stopping. If a contract is active when the trading socket drops, the bot re-subscribes the active contract(s) after reconnect and never opens replacement trades. V34.9.11 protects the active weighted pair and never opens replacement contracts during reconnect.

V34.9.11 dynamic weighted double-side mode

Entry rule: every qualifying V34 signal opens two trades together. The model-predicted direction receives a factor selected from its reliability-adjusted probability: 50–51% = 1.50×, 52–55% = 1.80×, 56–57% = 2.00×, and 58%+ = 2.20×. The opposite direction always uses exactly 1.00× Base.

Probability handling: the tier factor is based on the V34 selected-direction reliability-adjusted probability at entry. The V34 trade-qualification gate remains separate. The current V34 minimum-probability default remains unchanged at 60%, so with factory settings qualifying entries will normally use the 58%+ tier. Lowering the V34 minimum probability makes the lower tiers available.

Settlement rule: both contracts run independently to expiry. The factor is applied only to the model-predicted contract; there is no factor on the opposite contract. If both contracts lose, the bot treats the cycle as a BOTH-SIDE LOSS, resets Martingale to base, and starts the configurable delay before the next fresh model entry.

Important: this mode remains a simultaneous weighted pair, not sequential recovery. There are exactly two entry contracts per qualifying model signal and no third trade is created from a pair result. Global session-loss, max-trades, account, and connection safeguards remain active.

Opposite-direction recovery — legacy/non-weighted modes

Legacy recovery rule: non-weighted modes may use this setting. V34.9.11 does not use sequential recovery; its opposite contract is opened at entry at 1.00× base stake. Recovery does not wait for another model signal and does not require the model to agree with the recovery direction. The recovery stake is the actual losing stake × Loss progression factor, capped by Maximum loss stake cap. A recovery WIN ends the cycle and resets the next model stake to base. A recovery LOSS is a BOTH-SIDE LOSS: no third recovery is opened, Martingale is reset to base, and the bot waits for the configured delay in seconds before accepting a fresh model trade.

Recovery research: every live loss also starts a counterfactual opposite-direction shadow for the selected expiry. The CSV records recovery eligibility, model candidate, dynamic/calibrated/reliable probabilities, EV, trajectory, volatility, planned and actual recovery stake, actual recovery result/P&L, combined P&L of original + recovery, and the counterfactual shadow result. This is specifically included so future model updates can determine whether opposite recovery genuinely adds expectancy.

Optional warm-start history

Imported records are used only for rolling WR and persistence calculations. They are stored by market. Historical records from a different strategy are not proof of current behavior.

Account safety: select DEMO or REAL deliberately. REAL mode requires the explicit arming checkbox before START. The PAT is used only in page memory and is never written to localStorage. Market data is read from a separate public WebSocket; trading uses an authenticated account-scoped WebSocket for the selected account. If either connection drops, the bot stops rather than opening another trade.
